# Related-Work Literature Verification Checklist

**Purpose**: This document catalogues external academic and sports analytics literature topics that require formal verification, citation retrieval, and synthesis before drafting the full SSAC27 manuscript.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Citation Integrity Rule**: In accordance with project governance, no citations or references are fabricated. Each area listed below identifies the specific literature stream, key conceptual queries, and targets for verification against authoritative peer-reviewed databases and conference proceedings.

---

## 1. Counterpressing & Gegenpressing Empirical Analyses
* **Conceptual Focus**: Historical and tactical evolution of immediate transition pressing (e.g., German tactical literature on Gegenpressing, Dutch total football antecedents, modern high-tempo pressing).
* **Key Verification Questions**:
  - What prior studies have quantitatively evaluated counterpressing as an immediate post-turnover behavior versus general defensive pressing?
  - How have existing coaching texts or tactical analysts formally delineated the time window of a counterpress (e.g., the canonical "5-second rule" attributed to tactical theory)?
* **Verification Targets**: Peer-reviewed sports science journals (*Journal of Sports Sciences*, *International Journal of Sports Science & Coaching*), MIT SSAC research papers.

---

## 2. Pressing-Event Definitions & Event-Based Metrics
* **Conceptual Focus**: Industry-standard pressing metrics and their limitations.
* **Key Verification Questions**:
  - Original publications establishing Passes Per Defensive Action (PPDA), challenge intensity, and defensive action height.
  - Studies examining the sensitivity of event-based pressing metrics to team possession styles and game state.
* **Verification Targets**: StatsBomb conference presentations, Opta/Stats Perform research publications, academic sports analytics literature.

---

## 3. Possession Regain Studies
* **Conceptual Focus**: Quantitative modeling of ball recovery following turnovers.
* **Key Verification Questions**:
  - Prior definitions of "controlled possession regain" in soccer analytics literature.
  - Time-to-recovery distributions observed in professional matches.
  - Factors associated with successful regain in open play (e.g., pitch zone, player density, duel success).
* **Verification Targets**: Soccer analytics papers on turnover recovery, match analysis literature.

---

## 4. Spatial Soccer Analytics & Pitch Control
* **Conceptual Focus**: Mathematical modeling of space, player positioning, and spatial control.
* **Key Verification Questions**:
  - Foundational pitch control models (Spearman, Fernandez & Bornn, Link et al.).
  - Voronoi tessellations and player influence surfaces applied to defensive compaction.
  - Team centroid, dispersion, and convex hull metrics in team sport analysis.
* **Verification Targets**: MIT SSAC proceedings (Spearman 2018, Fernandez & Bornn 2018), *Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery*, *PLOS ONE*.

---

## 5. StatsBomb 360 & Freeze-Frame Computer Vision Applications
* **Conceptual Focus**: Research leveraging event-level broadcast freeze-frame data.
* **Key Verification Questions**:
  - Prior research papers utilizing StatsBomb 360 data for passing lane evaluation, defensive pressure, or line-breaking models.
  - Published validation studies on the coordinate accuracy of computer-vision extracted broadcast freeze frames.
* **Verification Targets**: StatsBomb Conference research papers (2021–2024), CMSAC papers, sports computer vision workshops (CVPR Sports, IJCAI).

---

## 6. Transition-Risk & Counterattack Modeling
* **Conceptual Focus**: Assessing threat and defensive vulnerability during opponent transition phases.
* **Key Verification Questions**:
  - Expected threat (xT), possession value models (PV), and transition xG applied to defensive vulnerability.
  - Space creation and passing velocity in counterattacking sequences.
  - Rest-defense (*Restabsicherung*) literature analyzing defensive structures maintained behind the ball during offensive phases.
* **Verification Targets**: MIT SSAC papers (Singh 2019, Karun et al.), football coaching literature on rest-defense.

---

## 7. Partial-Observation & Broadcast Truncation Methodology
* **Conceptual Focus**: Statistical handling of unobserved players outside the broadcast camera view.
* **Key Verification Questions**:
  - Statistical techniques for evaluating missingness in tracking/broadcast data (missing at random assumptions, camera sweep mechanics).
  - Prior sensitivity analyses testing camera visibility thresholds in broadcast sports datasets.
* **Verification Targets**: Computer vision in sports literature, spatial statistics literature on truncated viewing frustums.
