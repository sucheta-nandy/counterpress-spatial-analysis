# Carnegie Mellon Sports Analytics Conference (CMSAC) Research Presentation Submission

## Presentation Title
**When Does the Counterpress Work? Spatial Predictors of Possession Regain and Transition Risk in Elite Soccer**

## Authors
Research Team

---

## Abstract Summary

Counterpressing in elite association football creates an intense spatial dilemma: pressing immediately upon turnover can trap opponents deep in their half, but failed attempts expose extensive space behind the ball. Leveraging StatsBomb 360 freeze-frame tracking data across 50,366 counterpress actions (40,054 with 360 freeze frames) aggregated into distinct **counterpress episodes** across top international and domestic competitions (including the 2022 World Cup, UEFA Euro 2024, and the 2023/24 Bundesliga), we model the spatial conditions governing both rapid possession regain (within 5.0 seconds) and transition risk (within 15.0 seconds). 

We develop interpretable spatial features—such as local numerical superiority within 5, 10, and 15 StatsBomb coordinate units, nearest-teammate separation, and forward escape corridors—while controlling for camera visibility frustums. Employing grouped probability models evaluated across independent matches, we quantify the trade-offs between regain probability and transition vulnerability. Our findings provide practical thresholds for tactical decision-making, distinguishing scenarios where counterpressing is advantageous from those where regrouping into rest-defense is statistically optimal.
